# LaserX Design Website Scope

## Purpose

Promote Chris Hilton and LaserX Design as a premium design-and-build company serving manufacturing, fabrication, automation, machine building, and custom CNC work.

## Core services

- SolidWorks and AutoCAD design
- CAD drawings, parts, assemblies, and manufacturing documentation
- CNC plasma cutting
- custom metal parts and fabricated assemblies
- fixtures, poka-yoke, and production tooling
- prototype development
- automated machine design and building
- machine wiring, sensors, controls, and integration
- manufacturing process improvement
- TIG and MIG welding supporting designed systems and assemblies

## Primary proof project

The custom CNC plasma table will be developed as the first flagship case study. It will document the complete scope of work, including:

- concept and CAD development
- frame and gantry design
- fabrication and finishing
- automated water-table drain and fill system
- machine wiring and controls
- component integration
- assembly and testing
- continued software and controller development

## Target customers

- manufacturers needing fixtures or process improvements
- small businesses needing custom equipment
- product developers needing prototypes
- shops needing CAD, CNC-cut parts, or fabricated assemblies
- companies needing practical automation or machine retrofits
- individuals with technically serious custom projects

## Geographic position

LaserX Design is based in Muskegon, Michigan and can serve West Michigan locally while offering CAD, design, documentation, and selected development services beyond the immediate area.

## Content requirements

All claims must be grounded in real experience, actual projects, or services the company is prepared to provide. The website should promote Chris Hilton's capabilities without exaggerating credentials or inventing customer outcomes.
