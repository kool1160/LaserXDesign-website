# LaserX Design — Brand Positioning

## Core positioning

LaserX Design specializes in building autonomous systems, laser cutting, TIG welding, MIG welding, custom design, and industrial integration.

The company is positioned as a design-and-build partner that can take a project from concept through CAD, fabrication, wiring, controls, integration, testing, and finished implementation.

## Primary capabilities

- Autonomous and automated systems
- Custom machine design and machine building
- Laser cutting and CNC-based part production
- TIG welding
- MIG welding
- SolidWorks and AutoCAD design
- Custom fixtures, tooling, and production aids
- Mechanical and electrical integration
- Machine wiring, sensors, controls, and system integration
- Prototype development
- Production-ready fabricated assemblies

## Core message

LaserX Design does more than make parts. It designs, builds, connects, and proves complete systems.

## Supporting proof

The site should use documented work showing:

- CAD-to-floor fixture implementation
- Fixture-controlled repeatable production
- Dual-fixture and cobot-supported production setups
- TIG, MIG, aluminum, stainless, and production welding capability
- Dimensional verification and print discipline
- Prototype and heavy fabrication
- High-volume manufacturing support
- The custom CNC plasma table designed, fabricated, wired, assembled, and developed as a complete operating system

## Website positioning line

**Autonomous systems. Precision cutting. Welding. Design. Integration.**

## Extended website statement

LaserX Design brings together mechanical design, CAD, precision cutting, TIG and MIG welding, machine building, wiring, automation, and integration to create practical systems that are ready to work in the real world.

## Brand standard

The website must look and read like an advanced industrial design and automation company. It must not resemble a generic welding shop, contractor template, or hobby fabrication business.
